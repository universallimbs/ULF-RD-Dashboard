Baseline

For the baseline we are using an open source 3D printed arm called Waacs by e-nable. We will start our design edits and testing on this design and slowly reverse engineer from the best features Yalehand & Softfoot pro. Focus is on low resource setting and 6-12 year old terminal device.

YaleHand

We will reverse engineer key features from Yalehand to improve function, durability and usability: adaptive grasp, light weight, low force actuation and research validated performance.

Softfoot Pro

We will learn from Softfoot Pro to integrate the best mechanical features for simplicity and reliability: a proven, robust and field tested design with insights for low-resource use.

UL arm

A simplified, durable and affordable upper-limb prosthetic designed for low-resource settings and children aged 6-12 years. Accessible, easy to assemble, child-focused and built for real-world use.
