Prosthetic User Survey

This survey will help us design better, low-cost, body-powered upper-limb prosthetic devices.

Before you start

This survey is voluntary and anonymous — we do not collect your name or any identifying information unless you choose to share it. Your answers will only be used by Universal Limbs Foundation, a non-profit, to guide the design of an open-source pediatric prosthetic hand. Answers may be shared in aggregate (never individually) with our engineering and research partners. You can skip any question or stop at any time. This is not a medical service and does not replace advice from a clinician.
